What changed, and when

  1. Oct 26

    The real app on this page

    The screen-by-screen section now shows the iOS app itself, captured on the simulator, instead of the web demo’s phone: onboarding, the morning asks, the recovery dial with Keep going, Steady and Ease off, log pain, the daily log, insights, progress, the weekly report and settings. The demo at /learn is unchanged.

  2. Oct 23–25

    The app’s polish round

    Tick-arc recovery dial on Today with the morning asks first. Pain scale with words and graded colour. “Did it settle?” sheet with last night’s context and a one-line reaction. Progress charts with milestone flags and gains. The weekly report tells the week’s story. Three-step onboarding with goal chips. Settings with your comeback and data cards.

  3. Oct 21

    Warm neutrals

    The main screens were too bland, so the greys warm up: sand for labels, the line chart and your bars; clay for what raises risk, flare-ups and time past your line; sage for what lowers risk; stone for the people-like-you bars and the hourly ribbon. Today’s session sits on a sand card, with a soft warm light at the top of each screen. The opening stays grey and white.

  4. Oct 21

    A calmer palette

    Less techno, fewer colors. The opening’s charge and the coin’s light are now gray and white, not green. Charts, the week strip, signal bars and the live session dial are ink and gray. Color is kept for one thing: the day’s call (push, ease, rest).

  5. Oct 21

    The opening, the coin dial, more data

    Opening the app now plays a live 3D coin that floats, clicks into place and sends charge along printed traces to the corners. Today’s dial is the coin with its light ring as the gauge, plus a 7-day strip, what’s behind the call, and yesterday hour by hour. Learned gains a day-by-day chart.

  6. Oct 21

    The new logo, everywhere

    The ring-and-tick lockup replaces the text logo in the site header and footer, the demo page, the app's starting screen and on the coin. New favicon and home-screen icon.

  7. Oct 21

    The app, rebuilt to the flow

    Starting point, morning connect, Today with the new ring, guided sessions, check-in and "Got it", Learned with the week recap, and You. All in the brand system. This progress page.

  8. Oct 20

    Brand system and user flow

    Logo (the coin's ring with a tick at your line), colours, type, components and voice. The user flow for the iOS demo: setup, the daily loop, every screen.

  9. Oct 20

    The demo joins the site

    "How Kinn learns" moved onto this site at /learn, linked from the home page. Typed check-ins are read by Claude when the server has a key.

  10. Oct 17

    The learning engine

    Simulated wearers, learning on each phone from check-ins, federated averaging across phones, and honest scoring on people the model never saw.
