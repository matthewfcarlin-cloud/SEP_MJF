The app · as built, October 27, 2026

One loop a day.

RECO is the app that comes with the tape. Every morning it asks two questions and gives the knee one status for the day. One tap when something hurts. By the weekend it can tell you what came before a bad morning. Every screen below is the real app, running on sample data.

Status, every dayBend and straightening via the tapeNothing leaves the phone

01 · Every morning

Two quick questions, before anything else.

If you logged pain yesterday, RECO asks whether it settled overnight: yes, a bit, or no. Then a thirty-second check-in, pain and swelling. Both wait at the top of Today until you answer.

02 · The call for today

One status. The reason in plain words.

A recovery dial, then one of three calls against your own usual, never a clinical threshold. Under it, today's straightening and deepest bend from the tape. Nothing to interpret.

03 · When something hurts

One tap. RECO asks about it tomorrow.

Log pain is the only big button on Today. A number with a word on it, what you were doing, done. Until the tape logs your day for you, a daily log takes what you did and how hard, and shows what it adds against your usual.

04 · What it learns

What came before the bad mornings.

Insights puts it in one sentence: three of your last four flare-ups came after game days. Progress shows bend and straightening climbing toward the milestones. The weekly report tells the week in one line, ready to share with your physio.

05 · Yours

Three steps to start. Everything stays on your phone.

What happened, about you, what you are coming back to. Settings keeps your comeback in one line, a reminder for your physio's plan, and the data where it is. RECO tracks and shows; your physio makes the calls.
