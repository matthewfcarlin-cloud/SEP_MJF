RECO preorder terms

The plain version of what your deposit does.

It's non-binding

A preorder holds your place in line, so you get your RECO ahead of people who haven't preordered. You aren't committing to buy.

What you pay today

A deposit, which counts toward the price if you go ahead.

Refund anytime

Your deposit is refundable in full, for any reason, anytime before you pay the balance. Email recowearable@gmail.com from the address you ordered with.

If we're late

If your RECO hasn't shipped within of your preorder, we refund your whole deposit without you having to ask. If you'd rather keep your place, you can tell us and preorder again.

Price and shipping

The expected price is in total, including your deposit, and we expect to ship in . Both are estimates, not promises.

When yours is ready, we'll email you the final price and date. Nothing more is charged unless you say yes then. If you say no, or the price or date doesn't work for you, you get your whole deposit back.

What RECO is, and isn't

RECO is in development, so features may change before it ships. Images on our site are design renders, and the final product may look different. RECO tracks, shows and suggests. It is not a medical device, and it doesn't diagnose, treat, predict or prevent injury, or clear anyone to return to sport. Your PT's word overrides RECO's.
