What's in
the pilot.

  • Today

    One recovery number each morning, with yesterday's load next to your usual and how last night's pain settled.

  • Your plan

    The exercises your PT set, checked off as you do them through the week.

  • Log

    Add what you did, from practice to PT, and how hard it was. RECO turns it into a load you can compare.

  • Weekly report

    Your week in one line and day by day, ready to send to your PT as a PDF.
