23–30%

About 23 to 30% of young athletes have a second ACL injury after returning to sport. Most of a comeback happens between PT visits, where nobody is measuring.RECO is built for those days.

The tape

Athletic tape,
with a sensor inside.

Soft cotton kinesiology tape with a thin bend sensor across the kneecap window. A reusable coin twists into the top and records every bend, all day. When the tape is done, the coin moves to the next strip.

01 · TapeWear it all day

Applied like the KT tape you already know: anchor, strip, window over the kneecap.

02 · SensorMeasures every bend

Straightening and deepest bend, through workouts, stairs and sleep.

03 · CoinReusable, twist to lock

Satin aluminium and soft-touch black. Press it when your knee hurts.

04 · LightStatus at a glance

The ring glows teal while streaming, then your status colour after a sync.

How it works

Built for the days
your PT doesn't see.

Three things, every day. RECO tracks and shows. Your PT makes the calls.

Your best straightening and deepest bend, against your other knee and your own trend.
