Mission

Recovery,
measured.

Most knee recovery happens between appointments, with no data. Nearly 1 in 3 young athletes who return to sport after ACL reconstruction tear an ACL again within two years. Kinn is building a thin, wearable way to see how a knee is really recovering, for patients, physical therapists and the athletes trying to get back.

Paterno et al., Cincinnati Children's: 29.5% second ACL injury within 24 months of return to sport (n = 78).
