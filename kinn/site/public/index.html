The space between

Recovery doesn't happen in the clinic.

It happens at home. In the gym. During the exercises you aren't sure you're doing enough of.

You're trying to get stronger. To move more freely. To trust your body again. But progress isn't always easy to see, and knowing when to push or pull back isn't always obvious.

That's the gap we're exploring.

A new approach to recovery

Understand your movement. Rebuild your confidence.

We're exploring ways to make the physical changes happening during recovery easier to understand. By bringing movement sensing and useful feedback closer to your everyday routine, Kinn aims to help people make more sense of their progress between appointments.
