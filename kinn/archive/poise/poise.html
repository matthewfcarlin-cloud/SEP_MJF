UprightRounded

Why the tilt tracker stays green: when your shoulders round forward, your spine can stay almost upright. The angle barely changes, but the gap between your shoulder blades grows by 2–3 cm. Poise measures that gap directly, and only alerts after you stay rounded, so a quick reach or a deep breath doesn't set it off.
